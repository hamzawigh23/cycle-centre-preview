GIBRALTAR / SINCE 2011

Local roots.
A passion for cycling.

Founded by Robin Batchelor and Ian Walton, Cycle Centre is Gibraltar’s official Giant and Liv distributor. The shop moved to its larger Eaton Park premises in 2021.

Discover mountain, road, gravel, hybrid, folding and electric bikes, alongside children’s bikes, scooters and tricycles. Find clothing, parts, rentals and workshop support under one roof.
